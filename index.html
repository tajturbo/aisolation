01 — The problem

The knowledge exists. Fast, safe access to it doesn't.

Enterprise knowledge is scattered across wikis, Confluence and internal databases. Answers take hours, experts drown in repeat questions — and external AI services are off-limits for internal documents.

/01

Fragmented knowledge

Information is spread across systems: wikis, Confluence, file shares, incident history and ticket queues.

/02

External AI is restricted

Internal documents and employee queries must not leave the company perimeter or reach third-party providers.

/03

Access is not equal

Departments and roles have different rights. AI answers must respect them as strictly as the source systems do.

02 — The solution

One assistant. All your knowledge. Only what you're allowed to see.

We integrate a private LLM with your knowledge sources and corporate identity. An employee asks a question — the system answers only from documents they can access, with links to the originals.

RAG

Search across internal sources

Confluence, wikis and knowledge bases connected into one reasoning layer. Answers cite first-hand sources — every fact is verifiable.

SSO

Corporate identity

Integration with your existing SSO, LDAP or Active Directory. We don't create a parallel access model.

ACL

Permission-aware answers

Access rights are enforced during retrieval — before any content reaches the model's context, not just at display time.

LOG

Auditable by design

Who asked, which sources were used, what answer was generated. Logging scope, retention and access are defined by you.

03 — Security & data isolation

Leak protection is an architectural property, not a feature flag.

Isolated processing, access enforcement and channel control — across the entire solution, from document ingestion to the generated answer.

/01

Isolated processing loop

LLM, RAG, embeddings, search indexes and document parsing run inside your perimeter. No external dependencies, no telemetry.

/02

Rights-first retrieval

Restrictions are applied before content reaches the model. Employees see only what their role allows.

/03

Department isolation

Knowledge spaces, indexes and service accounts are segregated according to your policies.

/04

Egress control

Network policies restrict outbound connections. Integrations talk only to explicitly allowed systems.

/05

Sensitive data handling

Detection and masking of confidential data, output restrictions, integration with your corporate DLP stack.

/06

Audit without over-collection

You control log access, retention periods, content masking and deletion. The audit trail serves you — not a vendor.

04 — Infrastructure & hardware

Your infrastructure. We take care of it end to end.

Hardware is not our product — integration is. But the compute layer is part of the engagement: we size it, deploy it and keep it running.

ON-PREM Your servers, your racks

Deployment on your own hardware: physical control, optionally fully air-gapped. We handle GPU sizing, procuration guidance and capacity planning — you stay the hardware owner.

PRIV CLOUD Private or government cloud

Deployment in an in-country private or government cloud, under your jurisdiction — with full control over data and processing.

Everything stays inside. Documents, queries, answers, search indexes and logs never leave the agreed perimeter. Compute sizing is part of the service — from a single pilot node to a redundant production cluster.
